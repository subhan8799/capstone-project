## 2) Small Sitemap Sketch (Only What Earns Its Place)

Use this as your text sketch version, then hand-draw the same map on paper for the required photo.

Home (claim + strongest proof + action)
- Hero: clear claim in one sentence
- Proof strip: stack, results, reliability signals
- Featured work teaser with links to case studies
- Contact call-to-action

Work / Case Studies
- 2 to 4 projects, each with problem, approach, outcome, and repo link
- One highlighted case study with measurable result

About
- Short background
- Working values: clarity, reliability, iteration
- Tools and workflow habits

Contact
- Simple form or direct email
- One sentence on response time

Why each page earns its place:
- Home creates first belief and directs attention to one action.
- Work proves the claim with evidence.
- About builds trust and context quickly.
- Contact removes friction to the one action.

Pages intentionally excluded right now:
- Blog, testimonials, FAQ, and extra gallery pages.
Reason: they do not increase proof-to-action ratio for this first version.
